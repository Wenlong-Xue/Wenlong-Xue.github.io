About

Wenlong Xue

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellow working on MOF glasses, amorphous proton conductors and hybrid materials.

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellow, KU Leuven
Ameloot Group · cMACS, KU Leuven
Leuven, Belgium

Wenlong Xue works on the chemistry and processing of hybrid glass frameworks, MOF glasses, and amorphous proton conductors. The research connects coordination-network design with glass formation, mechanochemistry, melt processing, thin-film preparation, and structure-property analysis.

A central theme is the transformation of ordered crystalline frameworks into disordered but functional materials. These hybrid glasses combine local coordination order with processability, mechanical stability, and transport properties that are difficult to access in conventional crystalline solids.

Current work at KU Leuven focuses on directly synthesised amorphous networks, local structure and transport mechanisms, and routes for integrating proton-conducting hybrid glasses into energy and separation technologies.
